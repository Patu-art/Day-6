# Day 6 — Asset Guide

The current site already works with public reference imagery, but owner-supplied assets would make the demo feel much more authentic and client-ready.

## Best assets to add

1. `assets/images/always-the-same-hero.jpg`
   - Best use: hero image
   - Ideal: storefront, counter, sign, or a strong branded coffee shot
   - Shape: 4:5 portrait or vertical crop
   - Recommended size: at least 1400 px on the long edge

2. `assets/images/coffee-detail.jpg`
   - Best use: Harmony / coffee section
   - Ideal: espresso pour, latte, grinder, barista hands, branded cup
   - Shape: 4:5 portrait

3. `assets/images/bakes-detail.jpg`
   - Best use: La Chouquette / bake section
   - Ideal: pastry tray, croissant, baked goods on the counter
   - Shape: 4:5 portrait

4. `assets/images/daytime-interior.jpg`
   - Best use: daylight section
   - Ideal: real Always The Same seating/counter with daylight and people if possible
   - Shape: 4:5 or 3:4

5. `assets/images/team-or-jake.jpg`
   - Optional
   - Best use: local know-how / owner section
   - Ideal: natural working portrait, not a posed headshot
   - Shape: 4:5 portrait

6. `assets/videos/coffee-loop.mp4`
   - Optional but valuable
   - Best use: short motion moment between routine and pairing
   - Ideal: 5–10 seconds, muted-friendly, coffee being made
   - Shape: 9:16 or 4:5
   - H.264 MP4, preferably under 8–10 MB

## What to prioritise

If you can only get three files, get:

- one real Always The Same hero/storefront image
- one coffee/barista image
- one real daytime interior image

Those three will improve the site more than adding lots of generic photos.
